US orders federal agencies to remove China’s DeepSeek AI from their systems

The U.S. has mandated federal civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek products and all third-party services leveraging its technology, citing national security concerns. This move underscores the escalating geopolitical competition in advanced AI and critical infrastructure protection.

Why this is happening
  • Growing concerns about data security and foreign influence on critical government systems.
  • Intensifying US-China tech rivalry, particularly in the AI sector.
  • Governments are actively defining policies for AI use by state actors.
  • DeepSeek is a prominent Chinese AI developer, raising supply chain security questions.
Possible knock-on effects
  • Could lead to similar bans by other Western nations or alliances.
  • May accelerate domestic AI development and procurement by US agencies.
  • Likely to exacerbate tensions in the global AI technology market.
