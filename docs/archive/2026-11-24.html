Possible knock-on effects

  • Emerging trend of using AI to design and optimize hardware.

  • Aims to achieve significant performance and energy efficiency gains.
Possible knock-on effects
  • Could revolutionize traditional CPU design methodologies.
  • May lead to a new generation of highly energy-efficient AI hardware.
  • Likely to impact specialized computing for AI and complex simulations.
