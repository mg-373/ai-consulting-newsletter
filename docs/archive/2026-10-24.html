Pennsylvania Requires Labels on AI-Generated Ads

Pennsylvania Governor Josh Shapiro signed the Artificial Intelligence Deceptive Advertising Act into law, mandating disclosures for any ads "significantly modified or generated by artificial intelligence." This marks a significant step in regulating AI-generated content in advertising to ensure transparency for consumers.

Why this is happening
  • Rapid advancement of generative AI tools makes creating realistic fake content easier.
  • Concerns about consumer deception and misinformation are increasing.
  • Lack of clear federal or state regulations on AI content labeling existed prior.
  • Pennsylvania aims to set a precedent for transparency in AI advertising.
Possible knock-on effects
  • Could prompt other states to consider similar AI advertising legislation.
  • May increase compliance costs for advertisers and AI content creators.
  • Likely to influence future federal discussions on AI content regulation.

Possible knock-on effects

Possible knock-on effects
