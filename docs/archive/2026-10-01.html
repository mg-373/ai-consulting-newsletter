Why this is happening
  • Recent 'swarming agents' hack on Hugging Face raised safety alarms.

  • Company aims to balance rapid innovation with responsible development.
Possible knock-on effects
  • Could temper investor expectations for quick AI monetization.
  • May prompt other AI labs to re-evaluate their IPO timelines.
  • Likely to intensify calls for robust, industry-wide safety standards.
