Meta Removes Language Raising Concerns About AI Training Data Scraping

Meta has updated its commercial terms, removing language that previously suggested it could broadly scrape content from websites, including news sites, to train its AI models. This move comes after significant backlash and reflects growing sensitivity around data acquisition practices for large language models.

Why this is happening
  • AI models require vast amounts of data for training.
  • Content creators and news organizations raised IP and fair use concerns.
  • Industry scrutiny on data sources for AI training is intensifying.
  • Meta spokesperson claimed no change in crawling practices.
Possible knock-on effects
  • Could set a precedent for other AI developers' data policies.
  • May lead to renewed negotiations between tech and media companies.
  • Likely to influence future AI data legislation and guidelines.

StepFun Releases Step 5 Preview Open Weights: 600B MoE with 1M Context

StepFun has made its Step 5 Preview model available as open weights, a sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model built for long-horizon agentic work. It features 600B total parameters, 27B active per token, and an impressive 1M tokens of context, available on Hugging Face and ModelScope.

Why this is happening
  • Open-sourcing large, capable models benefits the entire AI research community.
  • Mixture-of-Experts architectures offer efficiency for large models.
  • Long context windows are essential for advanced agentic AI applications.
  • Developers need powerful foundational models for complex AI systems.
Possible knock-on effects
  • Could accelerate development of sophisticated AI agents and applications.
  • May foster new research into MoE scaling and long-context capabilities.
  • Likely to lower barriers for researchers and startups building large AI models.

  • Open-sourcing tools fosters community collaboration and accelerates development.

Possible knock-on effects

  • May improve trust and adoption of autonomous AI systems.
  • Likely to enable more complex and sensitive tasks for AI agents.
