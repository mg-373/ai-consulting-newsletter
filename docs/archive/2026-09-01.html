ChatGPT and Reddit Designated for EU's Toughest Online Safety Rules

OpenAI's ChatGPT and the social media platform Reddit are now subject to the European Union's stringent Digital Services Act (DSA). This designation as Very Large Online Platforms/Search Engines imposes significant new regulatory burdens to mitigate risks.

Why this is happening
  • The EU's DSA targets major online services to ensure user safety and content moderation.
  • ChatGPT's rapid growth and widespread use triggered its classification.
  • Reddit's large user base and content volume meet the VLOP criteria.
  • The designation requires platforms to address risks like harmful content and disinformation.
Possible knock-on effects
  • Will increase compliance costs and content moderation efforts for both companies.
  • May set a precedent for other global jurisdictions to implement similar AI regulations.
  • Could lead to changes in product features or data handling within the EU market.
