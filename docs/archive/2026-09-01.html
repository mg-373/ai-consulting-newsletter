Big Banks Demand Share of Lawyers' AI Savings, Pressuring Fees

Major financial institutions, including Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley, are reportedly pushing law firms to reduce their billing, citing the increased efficiencies and speed achieved through AI integration. This client-driven pressure signals a significant shift in how professional services are valued, as companies expect to benefit directly from their vendors' technological advancements.

Why this is happening
  • Law firms traditionally bill clients based on hours worked.
  • AI tools automate many routine legal tasks, reducing time.
  • Large corporate clients hold significant leverage over law firms.
  • Firms have heavily invested in AI for productivity gains.
Possible knock-on effects
  • Could set a precedent for client demands across professional services.
  • May accelerate adoption of AI for efficiency by consulting firms.
  • Likely to spark new value-based or outcome-driven pricing models.
