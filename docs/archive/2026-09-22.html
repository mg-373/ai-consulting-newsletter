  • The market is moving towards practical, enterprise-grade AI deployment at scale.
Possible knock-on effects
  • Could accelerate AI adoption across various industries due to lower barriers.
  • May intensify the price and performance war among leading AI providers.
  • Likely to enable new AI applications that were previously cost-prohibitive.
