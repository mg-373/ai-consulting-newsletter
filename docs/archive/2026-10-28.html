Why this is happening
  • AI models are increasingly accessible and used for various advice-seeking.

  • There is an ongoing debate about AI liability and safety guardrails.
Possible knock-on effects
  • Could lead to stricter regulations on AI's output, especially for sensitive topics.
  • May prompt AI developers to implement stronger content filtering or warnings.
  • Likely to increase public scrutiny and skepticism regarding AI's reliability.
